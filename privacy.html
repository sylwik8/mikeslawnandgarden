This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit this independent landscaping and outdoor-living information website or call the telephone number displayed on it.

This site provides information and helps generate telephone inquiries about landscaping, hardscaping, patios, retaining walls, and related services. The website operator does not necessarily provide those services directly.

Depending on how you use the website and how your inquiry is handled, the following information may be collected:

  • Information you choose to share when calling about a project, such as your name, contact details, ZIP code, or the type of service you need.

This list does not mean that every type of information is collected from every visitor.
