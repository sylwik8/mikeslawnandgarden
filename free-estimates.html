Before You Call

Having a few details ready can make your initial telephone enquiry more useful:

  • Your town or ZIP code
  • The type of landscaping or hardscaping work
  • Approximate dimensions or area
  • Any slope, drainage or access concerns
  • Your preferred timing and questions about budgeting

If a suitable independent provider is available, your call may be connected or referred for a discussion. The provider will explain its service area, whether site visits or estimates are offered, any charges, and its own process.

You are not obligated to proceed with a project simply because you call.
